# **Azrova SMP**

## **Peace Treaty**

We, the members of the Azrova SMP, agree to live together in peace, harmony, and mutual respect.

By accepting this treaty, we commit to resolving conflicts peacefully and responsibly rather than through unnecessary violence or destruction.

### **Terms of the Treaty**

1. **Peace & Non-Aggression**  
   Members shall not attack, kill, raid, or intentionally harm other members who are part of the treaty. If its wasnt on purpose or for goofing around The attacker should give all the items that are dropped or vanished back to the victim.  
2. **Respect**  
   All members shall respect each other's builds, items, territory, and personal belongings.  
3. **No Griefing**  
   Destroying, stealing from, or intentionally damaging another member's property is prohibited.
4. **Ownership & Credit**  
   Members shall not claim ownership, territory, or build credit for structures, designs, or projects created by other players.  
5. **Peaceful Resolution**  
   Any disagreements or conflicts shall first be discussed and resolved peacefully between the involved members.  
   Together we shall resolve the challenges together.  
6. **Mutual Assistance**  
   Members are encouraged to help one another with resources, protection, building, and other activities when reasonably possible.  
7. **Alliances**  
   Members who sign this treaty recognize each other as allies and agree to cooperate for the safety and prosperity of the SMP.  
8. **Breach of Treaty**  
   Anyone who deliberately violates the terms of this treaty may lose their status as an ally and face consequences decided by the members.

### **Declaration**

By agreeing to this treaty, we choose peace over conflict, cooperation over division, and unity over hostility. These terms also apply to your organisation members.

**Together, we build Azrova SMP.**
